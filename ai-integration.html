Working together

From a useful question to a working product.

I work across product strategy, AI interaction design and custom software development. We can shape an AI feature inside an existing product, connect it to your team’s workflow, or build the web platform it needs.

Possible starting points include a draft-and-review flow, recommendations people can question, or guided setup for a complex task. We start with one useful task and establish whether AI is the right tool.

  1. 01

    Map the work before the solution.

    We walk through the task with the people doing it: inputs, handoffs, repeated decisions and the cost of getting it wrong. We agree what improvement would look like and whether simpler rules or a clearer interface could solve it.

    You leave with: a focused problem, an initial scope and a baseline to compare against.

  2. 02

    Define access and human control.

    We map the systems and approved data the feature needs, who can access them and where information may go. We define what AI can suggest, what a person must review, and what happens when an answer is missing or wrong.

    You leave with: a workflow, data-access boundaries and acceptance criteria agreed with your team.

  3. 03

    Test the experience before the build.

    We prototype with representative, approved examples and involve the people who will use it. The test includes uncertain outputs, unavailable data, editing and recovery. Together we decide whether the evidence supports development or the idea needs to change.

    You leave with: a testable prototype, evaluation examples and a clear build decision.

  4. 04

    Build a pilot that fits your systems.

    I design and develop the agreed product experience, working with your team on integration and ownership. The scope covers the interface, connections to existing systems, permissions, review states and failure paths. We start with a bounded pilot, with someone responsible for handling issues and able to pause or roll back a release.

    You leave with: a working pilot, defined responsibilities and a practical rollout plan.

  5. 05

    Measure, improve and hand over.

    We compare task completion, review effort, output quality, response time and running cost with the baseline, using the measures agreed for your workflow. We decide what to improve before wider use, document how the feature works and name who owns evaluation, support and future changes.

    You leave with: a pilot assessment, operating guidance and a decision on the next release.

Questions before a first conversation

Before we begin.

What does AI integration mean for a business?

Connecting AI to the workflow you already run: the data you hold, the tools your team uses and the decisions people make. The aim is fewer manual steps and faster decisions, with a person still reviewing what matters.

Which workflow should we start with?

One that is repetitive, heavy on documents or data, and has a clear measure of success: time saved, fewer errors or faster answers. Discovery is where we find and rank those candidates together.

How do people stay in control of the AI?

AI collaboration settles that before anything is built: what the AI does, what a person reviews and what happens when it is wrong. Evaluation and error handling are designed in, not added later.

Do you also build the software?

Yes. Custom development takes the chosen use case from prototype to production, integrated with your systems, with clear ownership and a handover to your team.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Hungarian. This page is available in both.
