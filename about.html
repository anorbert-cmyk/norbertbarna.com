It starts with
a question.

Every product begins in a particular context. The useful questions often sit between what people need, what technology makes possible and what a team can sustain.

Making room for those questions gives the work direction. Before the interface, before the roadmap, there is a person trying to make something happen.

Understanding that moment is a place to begin.

02 / Perspective

A wider view.

Product work happens through people — across disciplines, viewpoints and constraints. The best outcomes come from shared understanding, not from any single perspective.

A wider view makes room for the whole system: the people using a product, the teams building it and the world it needs to work in.

The details matter. So does the space between them.
