# AI service content and internal linking — 2026-09-15

## Scope and editorial decision

The English `/ai-integration` and Hungarian `/hu/ai-integracio` pages remain one bilingual service offer. The approved Passage / Systems in Motion artwork, three-step animated ribbon, biography, five FAQs and closing footer remain intact. A new normal-flow `#workflow` chapter follows the ribbon. It explains five collaboration steps, their inputs, decisions and deliverables without extending the pinned scene.

This is a service page for a prospective client deciding whether to discuss a project. It is not a blog article or a claim that every possible engagement has already been delivered. First-person historical claims come from the existing owner-authored portfolio. The five-step process describes the proposed working method and deliverables to agree for each engagement.

The requested SearchFit `content-strategy` and `internal-linking` skills informed the audience/intent map, evidence ledger, content gaps and contextual links below. No keyword-volume, difficulty, backlink-authority, ranking or traffic dataset was available. Priorities are editorial judgments about relevance and visitor needs, not measured search demand.

4. Professional context: About, including the current BlackRock product/strategy scope.
5. Conversion: existing contact controls and preparation guidance; no new form or collection of personal data.

English and Hungarian topic phrases are descriptive candidates, not independently validated keywords. The offer and examples take priority over repetition of exact-match phrases.
