## Meta keywords policy

The owner asked for `<meta name="keywords">`. Google ignores the tag for ranking;
Bing reads it but treats stuffing as a spam signal. The tag therefore carries at
most eight honest, page-specific terms that the page already supports, mirrors the
long-tail column above and never adds a client, stack, figure or location the page
does not state. Case pages keep the same list in the Article `keywords` property
so the two never drift. `scripts/check-editorial-media.mjs` should enforce the cap
(presence allowed, at most eight comma-separated terms) instead of forbidding the tag.

## Structured data and answer engines

- Identity: one `Person` (`#person`) with `jobTitle` Product VP, `knowsLanguage`
  en and hu, and a `knowsAbout` list that mirrors the long-tail map. The
  `ProfilePage` name stays `Norbert Barna — Product VP` because the guards lock it.
- Case pages are `Article` with `BreadcrumbList`; service pages are `WebPage` plus
  `Service` with `provider` the home Person and `availableLanguage` en and hu; no
  `Offer`, `Review`, `AggregateRating` or `FAQPage` on service pages. Kineticare
  keeps its visible FAQ and matching `FAQPage`; Google retired FAQ rich results
  in May 2026, so this is parity, not a rich-result expectation.
- `dateModified` is set only when a page changes meaningfully; Article and
  ProfilePage values are full DateTimes with a timezone, `article:modified_time`
  and the sitemap `lastmod` follow them.
- Bilingual pairs (`/ai-integration` with `/hu/ai-integracio`, `/privacy` with
  `/hu/adatvedelem`) carry self-inclusive en, hu and x-default hreflang, self
  canonicals and `og:locale:alternate`. English-only pages need no hreflang.
- `llms.txt` is a factual summary (17 years, roles with exact date tuples, cases,
  services, languages, reuse guidance) and states which cases are not AI work.
- Long phrases are served by visible copy and the question-led blocks on the
  service pages, each answer standing on its own. No hidden text, no keyword lists
  in copy, no citation promise.

## Measured prioritization method

1. Record the same search type, canonical page group, country and device filters
   and complete date window for each comparison. Keep the export private.
2. Separate brand and navigation observations, service-intent observations and
   irrelevant queries. Do not optimize around an isolated noisy query.
3. Inspect the landing page and reader intent before changing titles or copy.
   Hidden query rows are unknown, not invented long-tail opportunities.
4. Prefer one useful correction to an existing page. A new page needs distinct
   intent, publishable first-hand evidence and enough material to answer the task.
5. Evaluate a change over comparable complete periods. Contact-button activity is
   intent, not a delivered email or a qualified lead.
