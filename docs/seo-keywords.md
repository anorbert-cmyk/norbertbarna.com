## Research-based editorial corrections

- Preserve the published AI workflow and case links. Publication, indexing,
  impressions, clicks and qualified enquiries are separate states.
- The shared home/AI/privacy social graphic is
  `/assets/images/og/forest-olive-folds.jpg` (1200×630). It is decorative geometry.
  ProfilePage uses that page image; the Person object does not claim it as a portrait.
- The current site loads consent-gated PostHog analytics scripts. A former “GA4
  still needs installing” note is historical, not the current measurement plan.
  Code presence alone does not prove ingestion or qualified-enquiry attribution.
- The September 4 apex 404 finding needs its dated live recheck, not a timeless
  “open issue” label. Current HTTP/redirect evidence belongs to the private audit.
- Case dates describe actual content history. Change dates only when the page
  changes meaningfully; do not refresh every date to manufacture recency.
- Titles and descriptions are editorial summaries, not character-count ranking
  formulas. Keep the existing design/brand contract and natural language.
- No keyword density target, meta-keywords tag, paid link package, citation promise
  or extra schema as a substitute for useful visible content.
- The existing FAQ answers remain useful to readers. Google discontinued FAQ rich
  results from May 7, 2026; retaining accurate FAQ markup is not an entitlement to
  a rich result. [Google changelog](https://developers.google.com/search/updates#may-2026)

## Measured prioritization method

1. Record the same search type, canonical page group, country/device filters and
   complete date window for each comparison. Keep the underlying export private.
2. Separate brand/navigation observations, service-intent observations and irrelevant
   queries. Do not optimize the page around an isolated noisy query.
3. Inspect the actual landing page and reader intent before changing titles or copy.
   Page aggregation and property aggregation can differ; do not add them as unique
   demand. Hidden query rows are unknown, not invented long-tail opportunities.
4. Prefer one useful correction to an existing page. A new page requires distinct
   intent, publishable first-hand evidence and enough material to answer the task.
5. Evaluate a chosen change over comparable complete periods. No forecast or “quick
   win” label is supported by the present sample. Contact-button activity is intent,
   not a delivered email or qualified lead.
