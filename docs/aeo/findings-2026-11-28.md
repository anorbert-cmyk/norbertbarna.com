# Answer engine visibility: second measurement round, 28 November 2026

Gemini and Claude on the same fixed questions, plus a look at who is cited instead. Method and files: `docs/aeo/README.md`. Scores: `docs/aeo/score-2026-11-28.md` (Gemini and Claude) and `docs/aeo/score-2026-11-27.md` (the first three engines).

## What was added

- **Gemini** (consumer app, logged out, country by language): all 90 questions.
- **Claude** (Haiku 4.5 with web search): the 78 questions that are not about the site itself. The 12 brand questions were left out to keep the cost down; the site is not the question there.
- **Where the site stands in an answer** and **share of voice** are now part of the score script and of every score file.

## Results

| Engine | Answers | Cite the site | Of which non-brand |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gemini | 90 | 7 (7.8%), all on brand questions | 0 of 78 |
| Claude | 78 | 0 | 0 of 78 |

Across all five engines the site is cited in 1 of 390 answers to questions that are not about the site itself.

1. **Gemini mostly answers without sources.** 56 of its 90 answers carry no link at all, 51 of 78 on non-brand questions. It cites the site when it is asked about it (7 of 12 brand answers, first source in 6 of the 7), including the Kineticare case study for the question who built the platform. For everything else there is nothing to be cited in.
2. **Claude with search cites, and spreads its sources widely.** 77 of 78 answers have sources; no domain dominates. The Hungarian answers cite Hungarian provider sites in 18 of 26 cases, so a Hungarian page that answers the question has a real chance there.
3. **Share of voice is fragmented.** In the first round, on the 234 non-brand answers, the top domains are official sources (digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu 12.0%, eur-lex.europa.eu 9.8%, linkedin.com 8.1%) on the legal and AI Act questions. Small providers sit at 2 to 3% each (automating.hu, aiboost.hu, composite.hu, webcraft.hu). The site stands at 0.4%. A share of 2 to 3% would already be a top 20 domain.
4. **When the site is cited it is first.** Baseline: first source in 21 of 31 answers, mean position 1.6. Gemini: first in 6 of 7.

## Notes on the engines

- The Claude endpoint is not a consumer app. The larger Sonnet model ignored the forced web search in most calls and ran out of tokens on the rest, so those calls (about 60 of them) were discarded. Haiku 4.5 with a 1024 token cap searched every time. Hungary is not an accepted search country; Hungarian questions run without one.
- Gemini cites one page several times with different `#:~:text=` fragments. The collector now counts a page once.
- ChatGPT and Gemini are consumer-app answers (cloro), Google AI Mode is a scraped result page, Perplexity and Claude are model APIs. The two kinds are not compared with each other here.

## What this means

The site is understood and cited when someone asks about it. For topic questions the limit is still being in the candidate set, which is indexing and authority, not wording. The twelve new articles and the indexing requests in Search Console are the lever; the next measurement (early January 2027, all five engines) shows whether it moved.
