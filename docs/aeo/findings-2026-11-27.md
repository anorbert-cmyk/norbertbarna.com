# Answer engine visibility: findings, 27 November 2026

Method and files: `docs/aeo/README.md`. Score: `docs/aeo/score-2026-11-27.md`.

## The number

90 questions, three engines (Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity), 270 answers.

| Question type | Answers | Cite the site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand (who is Norbert Barna, what is the site, who built Kineticare) | 36 | 30 (83.3%) |
| Commercial (who offers this service) | 60 | 1 (1.7%) |
| Informational (questions the articles answer) | 174 | 0 (0.0%) |
| All | 270 | 31 (11.5%) |

The three engines and the three languages behave alike: between 10% and 13% each, all of it from brand questions.

## What the answers show

1. **The entity is understood.** Asked about the person or the site, all three engines answer from the site itself: home, About, the service pages, the case studies, the articles. Person schema, llms.txt and the case studies do their job. The one gap: “What is barnanorbert.com?” gets a correct summary in Google AI Mode without a link, and ChatGPT in English says it found nothing reliable.
2. **The articles are not in the candidate set.** For 58 informational questions no engine cited the site once, including the questions that an article answers almost word for word. Search Console confirms the cause: in the last 30 days only 38 of the 71 sitemap URLs that existed before this change were shown at all, most articles and the Hungarian and German service pages never. A page that the search index behind an engine does not return cannot be cited, however well it is written.
3. **Small sites do get cited.** The cited sources for commercial and price questions are mostly small agencies and solo consultants (for example rmwebstudio.hu, webcraft.hu, aiboost.hu, automating.hu, ki-beratung-deutschland.de, lukas-fischer.de). Authority helps on legal questions, where EUR-Lex, the Commission, IHK and Bundesfachstelle take everything, but elsewhere the field is open to a site that is indexed and answers the question directly.
4. **Hungarian has the most room.** For eight Hungarian questions (custom or template, redesign or rebuild, AI in customer service, AI hallucination, regulated MVP, AI cost, questions for an AI partner, classic automation or AI) the engines cited English-language pages almost exclusively, because they found no usable Hungarian source. Three of these had no Hungarian article on the site.
5. **The one commercial citation shows the pattern.** ChatGPT cited `/ai-integration` for “AI integration consultants in Hungary who work in English”: a narrow query where place, language and service are all stated on the page.
6. **Related articles were a dead end.** Every article listed the same two newest articles. Six articles had one inbound link from page content in the whole site.

## What was changed

- **Twelve new articles**, each written against the engines’ own answers to its question: six English (AI in customer service, custom website or template, AI for small business in 90 days, website redesign or rebuild, which process to automate first, what AI integration cost depends on), four Hungarian (AI hallucination, regulated MVP, choosing an AI partner, what AI development cost depends on), two German (choosing an AI provider, what AI consulting cost depends on). Every topic except the three legal ones now exists in all three languages, linked with hreflang.
- **Related articles by topic.** Each article now links the three closest articles of its language (same service, shared tags), balanced so that every article is linked from several others.
- **`/llms-full.txt`**: the full text of every article in one Markdown file with canonical URLs and dates, named in `llms.txt`.
- **The measurement itself**: fixed question set, result file, score script with comparison against the previous run.

## What is not solved by on-site work

- Indexing. Requesting indexing in Search Console for the never-shown pages (service pages first) is the single most effective step left, and only the owner can do it.
- Authority. No external links yet; the prepared outreach and guest pitches are unsent.
- The legal topics (GDPR and AI, accessibility law) in Hungarian and English were left out on purpose: official sources take every citation there, and the texts would need a lawyer.

## Next measurement

Four to six weeks after deploy, with the same 90 questions. The numbers to watch: informational and commercial citations (now 1 of 234), and Hungarian in particular.
