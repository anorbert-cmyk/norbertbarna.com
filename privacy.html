Analytics and privacy

Magyarul

This notice explains the current inactive analytics setup and search-performance reporting on this portfolio. Norbert Barna is responsible for this website. For a privacy request, use the Email button at the bottom of the page.

Visitor analytics is currently off

The website does not send page views or Email-button clicks to PostHog. Session recordings, heatmaps and automatic click collection are not active. There is no analytics prompt to dismiss: browsing and contacting me work without measurement.

No analytics browser storage

The inactive integration does not read or write consent preferences or analytics session identifiers. A previously saved choice does not enable measurement in this release. No advertising cookies or advertising platform connection is added by this integration.

Search performance and site delivery

Google Search Console provides aggregated search-performance reports for this domain. Those reports are also connected to a dedicated PostHog EU project; this server-side reporting connection does not install a visitor tracker in your browser. See PostHog's privacy notice.

The website uses Railway hosting and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those services receive the network requests needed to deliver the website, independently of optional analytics. A receiving service necessarily sees a network address when handling a request. Disabling analytics does not remove these existing delivery requests.

Before any future activation

Optional visitor measurement will require a separate release, confirmed processor arrangements and retention terms, and an updated notice explaining the purpose, data and retention periods before asking for consent. It will not be activated by an old browser preference.

Privacy questions

You can ask about access, correction, deletion or other applicable data rights using the Email button. You may also contact your data-protection authority. Clicking Email opens your email application; it does not send a message automatically.
